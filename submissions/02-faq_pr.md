## Question

What minimum score is required to pass the final project and qualify for the certificate?

## Answer

For the current AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp cohort, the minimum score required to pass the final project is 10 points.

You can check the current pass mark on the course dashboard under "Project outcomes." This requirement may change for future cohorts, so always refer to your cohort's dashboard for the most up-to-date information.

Passing the final project is one part of certificate eligibility. You also need to complete your assigned peer reviews, which is a separate requirement regardless of your project's score.
